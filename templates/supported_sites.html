What sites are supported by this web tool? This web deployment of YT Downloader is strictly dedicated to YouTube content (including Standard Videos, YouTube Shorts, YouTube Music, and YouTube Playlists). While the underlying open-source yt-dlp engine contains extractors for over 1,000 domains, our public web server enforces a strict YouTube-only allowlist to ensure bulletproof SSRF (Server-Side Request Forgery) security, prevent server abuse, and maximize transcoding performance.

  • Strict YouTube Allowlist: Validates domains against youtube.com, youtu.be, and music.youtube.com.
  • Full Format Support: Single videos, Shorts, mobile links, and playlists up to 50 items.
  • Self-Host for 1000+ Sites: Run our open-source codebase locally to unlock generic extractor support for other platforms.

Why We Enforce a Dedicated YouTube Allowlist

Many generic video converters claim to download from hundreds of platforms indiscriminately. However, in production web engineering, accepting unconstrained arbitrary URLs creates grave security risks:

  • SSRF Defense: Restricting network traffic to certified Google/YouTube CDN hostnames guarantees that attackers cannot exploit stream extractors to probe local host ports (e.g. 127.0.0.1, AWS/cloud metadata services, or intranet microservices).
  • Dedicated Codec Pipelines: By focusing our transcoding workers exclusively on YouTube's DASH stream formats (VP9, AV1, and Opus), we ensure deterministic FFmpeg conversion times and predictable server load.
  • Zero Broken Extraction Guarantees: Third-party sites frequently change their HTML layouts and obfuscate tokens. By focusing our testing on YouTube, we maintain a 99%+ extraction success rate.

Strict Limitations & What We Do NOT Support

In adherence to ethical software engineering and copyright protection laws:

  • DRM-Protected Services: We do NOT support and will never attempt to decrypt services protected by Widevine, FairPlay, or PlayReady DRM (e.g. Netflix, Amazon Prime, Spotify, Disney+, Hulu).
  • Paywalled or Private Content: Videos requiring paid subscription tiers, patron access, or account login credentials are not accessible.
  • Non-YouTube Domains: Other domains (e.g. Vimeo, TikTok, SoundCloud) are blocked on this public instance. If you need multi-platform extraction, deploy your own instance using our Self-Hosting Guide.
  • Region-Blocked Content: We do not operate circumvention proxies or automated IP hopping to bypass platform geolocation restrictions.

Frequently Asked Questions

What should I do if a link from a supported site fails?

Streaming platforms occasionally update their video player cipher or API format. Our yt-dlp upstream dependency publishes regular updates to accommodate these changes. You can also inspect the URL in our Video Info Checker to see if metadata is readable.

Can I test multiple links at once?

Yes. Use our Bulk Audio or Bulk Video tools to paste batches of up to 20 URLs.

Why does YT Downloader reject links to private videos?

Private and unlisted videos requiring user account authentication cannot and should not be accessed without the creator's authorization. We do not accept user passwords or session cookies to access restricted content.

Can I suggest a new streaming site for testing?

Yes. You can contact our team through the Contact & Support page or submit extractor pull requests directly to the open-source yt-dlp project.
