Frequently Asked Questions

Why does a 4K download take longer than 1080p?

4K video files contain four times more pixel data than 1080p. The server must download both large files (often over 200MB) from YouTube and multiplex them with FFmpeg before serving the completed file.

What media player is best for playing 4K videos on PC or Mac?

We recommend VLC Media Player, mpv, or IINA (on macOS). Both utilize your computer's dedicated graphics hardware to decode 4K 60fps VP9 and AV1 streams effortlessly.

Does YT Downloader compress or downscale 4K files?

No. Unlike many web downloaders that secretly downscale 4K streams to 1080p or 720p to save server bandwidth, YT Downloader serves the genuine 2160p video stream directly from YouTube's CDN.

Can I play 4K videos on a phone?

Modern smartphones (iPhone 11 and later, Galaxy S20 and later, Google Pixel 5 and later) can decode 4K 60fps video with hardware acceleration. However, on small screens, 1080p looks virtually indistinguishable and saves significant storage.
