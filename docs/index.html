❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Why is there no advertisement or subscription fee?

YT Downloader is an open-source, non-profit project created for education, digital archiving, and fair-use media preservation. We do not partner with ad networks or sell user telemetry.

Why does 1080p and 4K video require FFmpeg merging?

YouTube does not host combined audio/video files for resolutions above 720p. Instead, it serves video-only and audio-only streams via adaptive DASH protocol. We use FFmpeg to seamlessly multiplex these streams into a single playable MP4 file.

What is the difference between MP3 and native M4A?

YouTube natively stores audio in AAC (inside M4A) or Opus formats. When you choose M4A, the stream is saved without re-encoding, preserving exact original sound quality. MP3 re-encodes the stream for maximum compatibility across older car stereos and media players.

How does the 24-hour retention policy work?

Whenever a download job completes, the file is saved to transient server disk space. An automated cron background process scans the download directory and permanently removes any file older than 24 hours to safeguard user privacy.

Is it legal to download YouTube videos?

Downloading content that you own, that is explicitly licensed under Creative Commons, or that resides in the public domain is generally legal under fair use principles. Downloading copyrighted commercial media without authorization is a violation of copyright law and YouTube's Terms of Service. For a balanced overview, read our Fair Use & Archival Policy.
